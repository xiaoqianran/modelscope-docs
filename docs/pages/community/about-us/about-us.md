# **What is ModelScope?**
**ModelScope aims to build the next-generation open-source Model-as-a-Service sharing platform, providing flexible, easy-to-use, and cost-effective one-stop model services for AI developers, making model applications simpler!**

The ModelScope community was officially launched in early November 2022. It is an open-source model community and innovation platform initiated by Alibaba's Tongyi Lab in collaboration with the CCF [Open Source Development Technical Committee](https://www.ccf.org.cn/kyfzwyh/). The ModelScope community partners with domestic AI industry collaborators and academic institutions to promote the prosperity of the model application ecosystem through open community collaboration, building open-source deep learning models and innovating open-source model service technologies.

We hope to gather industry-leading pre-trained models, reduce redundant R&D costs for developers, and provide a more environmentally friendly, open-source AI development environment and model services to support the development of green "digital economy" initiatives. The ModelScope platform will provide various high-quality models in an open-source manner, allowing developers to freely experience and download them on the platform.

If you share our vision, we welcome your attention and support. We encourage and support individual or enterprise developers to contact us. The platform will provide you with better support services to jointly contribute to the broader AI community.

# **What We Offer**

- **Rich Collection of Pre-trained SOTA Models**

Competitive state-of-the-art (SOTA) models covering multiple domains including NLP, CV, and Audio, plus industry-leading multimodal large models—all available for free download and use.

- **Diverse and Open Datasets**

A collection of popular public datasets from industry and academia, including specialized domain datasets contributed by Alibaba Group for you to explore.

- **Model Inference Capability with One Line of Code**

Provides local inference interfaces based on models and online model inference prediction services, enabling developers to quickly verify and use models.

- **Build Custom Industry Models with Ten Lines of Code**

Fine-tune pre-trained models with just a dozen lines of code (finetune), allowing developers to quickly build custom industry-specific models based on industry datasets.

- **Ready-to-Use Online Development Platform**

One-click access to online Notebook training platforms, integrated with official images to eliminate environment setup hassles, connected to powerful cloud computing resources for convenient interactive programming experiences.

- **Flexible Model Frameworks and Deployment Options**

Compatible with mainstream AI frameworks for better model migration; multiple model training and service deployment options provide more autonomous and controllable choices.

- **Comprehensive Educational Content and Technical Resources**

Provides friendly, high-quality tutorial content and an open community atmosphere to help developers learn and grow. (Content is continuously being updated—stay tuned!)

# AI Ethics Initiative

Dear ModelScope users of the Institute for Intelligent Computing,

Thank you for choosing the artificial intelligence models or services provided by the Institute for Intelligent Computing. We will strive together with you to develop responsible AI and jointly create a better future.

In our practice of AI ethics governance, the Institute for Intelligent Computing adheres to the fundamental principles of **human-centeredness, inclusivity and integrity, safety and reliability, privacy protection, trustworthiness and controllability, and open collaboration**. We are steadily advancing our AI ethics governance work and have achieved positive results in practice. Practicing the technological development philosophy of "good technology" and enhancing the overall and long-term well-being of humanity has always been our pursuit. Achieving AI ethics governance requires participation and collaborative exploration from the entire society, so we issue this initiative to all our users.

1. We commit to actively adhering to the above principles and ensuring that AI is researched and applied in an ethical manner. However, responses from members of society are also crucial. Therefore, we hope you will:

(1) Comply with relevant national laws, regulations, and policies, and do not use the models or services on this platform for illegal purposes such as data theft, telecommunications fraud, or creating and spreading false information. Consciously resist, report, and denounce unlawful activities.

(2) Practice social ethics, actively uphold public morality and good customs, and adhere to moral principles.

(3) Carefully read and comply with the platform's "User Agreement" and "Privacy Policy," do not maliciously crack, develop, or abuse the platform's models or services, and respect others' privacy and data protection.

2. For enterprise users, we also hope you will:

(1) Exercise self-discipline and responsibility, actively establish AI ethics guidelines, set up ethics review mechanisms, and strengthen safety and compliance reviews.

(2) Properly guide your customers to legally and ethically use the products or services you develop and provide externally based on the platform's models or services.

(3) Innovate through sharing and collaborative exploration, working together to build a safe and healthy environment for AI research, development, and application.

3. We encourage you to use the platform's models and services for public welfare purposes that enhance human well-being, such as scientific and technological exchange, educational resource sharing, social security assurance, and environmental and ecosystem protection, helping more people benefit from the light of technology.

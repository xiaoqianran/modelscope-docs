**Welcome to ModelScope Nexa!** This is an open-source collaborative community dedicated to providing scientists, AI engineers, and researchers in fundamental scientific fields worldwide with services for discovering, using, and exchanging scientific AI resources.

In recent years, artificial intelligence has achieved remarkable results in scientific research, driving the emergence of the concept of "AI-driven scientific research" (AI for Science, AI4S). The 2024 Nobel Prizes in Physics and Chemistry were both awarded to scientists in the field of artificial intelligence, further highlighting the immense potential of AI4S. This has become a global consensus in the scientific and technological community and has prompted an increasing number of government agencies to provide policy-level support.

ModelScope firmly believes that AI's positive impact on human society will be comprehensive—not only reshaping our work and lifestyle but also accelerating the pace of human scientific exploration. To this end, ModelScope actively participates in building the open-source ecosystem and infrastructure for AI for Science. In collaboration with NovaInspire and other platforms, we are officially launching ModelScope Nexa and sincerely invite researchers and developers to follow and participate in jointly advancing scientific intelligence.

# Feature Overview

ModelScope Nexa builds upon ModelScope's existing AI infrastructure to create an AI community where scientists can easily access "out-of-the-box" solutions and efficiently engage in "open-source collaboration." On one hand, we systematically aggregate AI resources centered around scientific themes, including models, datasets, and applications. On the other hand, we provide SDKs, Notebooks, and other tool products to support the development, building, hosting, and exchange of scientific models, scientific datasets, and scientific applications. Currently, the Nexa section is primarily divided into three main areas: Models, Datasets, and Collections, with Collections currently under development.

## Homepage (Models)

### ModelScope Nexa: Disciplinary Classification System (Under Development)

AI for Science encompasses various disciplines and fields of scientific research. From the perspective of an open-source community, we actively provide community users interested in AI for Science with a clear and comprehensive scientific AI classification system and task framework.

Starting from fundamental scientific disciplines, we have established a basic disciplinary classification system comprising the following four major areas:

- **Life Sciences**: covering subfields such as biology, medicine, healthcare, organs, proteins, RNA, DNA, etc.

- **Earth Sciences**: including subfields such as prediction, analysis, energy and power, observation, remote sensing, astronomy, etc.

- **Materials Sciences**: including subfields such as molecular structure, material structure, chemical reactions, organic molecules, scientific computing, etc.

- **Social Sciences**: including subfields such as history, law, economics and finance, social sentiment, etc.

Currently, the community's classification system is still in its early stages. The disciplinary classification system for ModelScope Nexa continues to expand and develop, and we welcome your suggestions and contributions to help build this community together.

### Creating Scientific Models

On the Nexa homepage, you can quickly create a scientific model by clicking the "Create Model" button.

![alt text](./_resources/create_model.png)

During the creation process, you'll need to fill in essential basic information, including:
- Model English ID
- Owner
- License: Open-source license type
- Public visibility
- Disciplinary field:
    - Primary field: Life Sciences / Earth Sciences / Materials Sciences / Social Sciences
    - Secondary field: Tags related to the primary field

Additionally, you can optionally provide a display name, model description, custom tags, model cover image, and README file at this stage. We encourage you to refer to the [Model Card](../models/model-card.md) documentation when creating your model to provide detailed model introductions, usage examples, and other relevant information.

## Datasets

The development of AI for Science fundamentally depends on the open-source construction of AI datasets. High-quality datasets form the foundation for model training, optimization, and inference, with their quality, quantity, and diversity directly impacting model performance. Scientific research data comes in diverse forms (including experimental, observational, and simulation data), and different scientific disciplines exhibit significant variations in data categories and complexity. The AI4S field urgently needs to build a more open ecosystem of high-quality open-source datasets. We believe that by open-sourcing more complex, multi-modal, and multi-layered scientific datasets (such as high-dimensional nonlinear data), we can foster the flourishing development of more promising scientific models and help humanity achieve more valuable scientific discoveries.

ModelScope Nexa's dataset classification system aligns with the model classification system. You can switch to the "Datasets" page via the top navigation bar and conveniently filter and search for datasets of interest.

![alt text](./_resources/dataset_list.png)

### Creating Scientific Datasets

In the upper-right corner of the Datasets page, click "Create Dataset" to quickly create a scientific dataset through the interface.

![alt text](./_resources/create_dataset.png)

The fields required during dataset creation are largely similar to those for scientific model creation. To help community users better understand dataset details and use cases, we encourage you to refer to the [Dataset Card](../datasets/dataset-card.md) documentation when creating your dataset, providing comprehensive basic information and enabling dataset preview functionality.

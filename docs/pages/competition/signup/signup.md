## Competition Registration
![image.png](https://intranetproxy.alipay.com/skylark/lark/0/2022/png/29482/1670206270916-bf0cfcf2-5f23-4e89-8207-560bbc2b910d.png#clientId=u3f744faa-3c7c-4&crop=0&crop=0&crop=1&crop=1&from=paste&height=1362&id=u27060fd5&margin=%5Bobject%20Object%5D&name=image.png&originHeight=1362&originWidth=2826&originalType=binary&ratio=1&rotation=0&showTitle=false&size=2215582&status=done&style=none&taskId=ubb29b9ec-c754-4e27-b82d-108b1d10d6d&title=&width=2826)

Registration requires logging in first. If you don't have a ModelScope community account yet, you need to register an account and log in. New registered users receive a limited-time gift of 100 hours of free GPU computing power, providing more resource support for your competition!
![image.png](https://intranetproxy.alipay.com/skylark/lark/0/2022/png/29482/1670206315362-d67fa032-7005-483a-8ab0-82fe119a5c91.png#clientId=u3f744faa-3c7c-4&crop=0&crop=0&crop=1&crop=1&from=paste&height=1342&id=uf4438723&margin=%5Bobject%20Object%5D&name=image.png&originHeight=1342&originWidth=2674&originalType=binary&ratio=1&rotation=0&showTitle=false&size=2296605&status=done&style=none&taskId=uad873f50-16f1-43d8-b9db-6b384428134&title=&width=2674)

After logging into the ModelScope platform, click "Register Now" again. You will need to submit a valid email address to allow the competition organizers to contact you via email. Please ensure your email address is filled out correctly. The organization you belong to represents the organization or enterprise you represent, which will be displayed on the leaderboard.
Before registering, please ensure you have carefully read and understood the "[Participant Agreement](https://modelscope.cn/protocol/participant-agreement)"

After correctly filling in the email address and organization, you can successfully register. At this point, you can choose to form a team to compete or not form a team and compete as an individual. It should be noted that: before submitting competition results, you can freely form teams, but once results are submitted, your member information will be locked, and changes to team member information during the competition will not be allowed. It is recommended to invite friends to participate together before the competition and confirm the list of members.

## Forming Teams
If you choose to form a team, you can click the "Form Team" button and confirm your team name.

![image.png](https://intranetproxy.alipay.com/skylark/lark/0/2022/png/29482/1670206793509-660b65f2-7b20-4072-8107-a278cf12563e.png#clientId=u3f744faa-3c7c-4&crop=0&crop=0&crop=1&crop=1&from=paste&height=1178&id=u73682ce2&margin=%5Bobject%20Object%5D&name=image.png&originHeight=1178&originWidth=2214&originalType=binary&ratio=1&rotation=0&showTitle=false&size=1079191&status=done&style=none&taskId=ue42ce22f-1ce6-4c11-9653-7947947db18&title=&width=2214)

Note that different competitions have limits on the number of team members, so please ensure your team does not exceed the upper limit. Click "Add Member" and enter the member's username to successfully add them. (Username is the member's login name, which can be viewed in the top right corner of the website)

Please ensure that the members you invite have also completed the "Register Now" operation. Otherwise, the following error message will appear.

If the other party has also successfully registered, enter the member's username here to successfully add them. (In this phase, the team leader can add or remove members, and members cannot voluntarily leave the team. Please ensure offline communication is completed before forming a team.)

## Disbanding Teams
Before submitting results, if you no longer wish to form a team, the team leader must initiate disbanding the team. After disbanding, members will revert to single-person competition mode and can form a new team.

![image.png](https://intranetproxy.alipay.com/skylark/lark/0/2022/png/29482/1670210571479-ecdeb62a-b44d-497e-957c-4d61feceeff7.png#clientId=ua070116f-fe12-4&crop=0&crop=0&crop=1&crop=1&from=paste&height=671&id=u0b50fd13&margin=%5Bobject%20Object%5D&name=image.png&originHeight=1342&originWidth=2746&originalType=binary&ratio=1&rotation=0&showTitle=false&size=1218085&status=done&style=none&taskId=udf2fab89-2c16-4b70-9e7b-31b6ffe7e17&title=&width=1373)

In the same competition, a participant cannot join multiple teams. If you no longer wish to continue participating in this team, inform the team leader, who will manually remove the member.

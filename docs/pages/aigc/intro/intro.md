Welcome to Civision on ModelScope! This is a one-stop AI creation platform dedicated to perfectly blending creativity and technology.

>You can find Civision directly in the navigation bar on the ModelScope homepage.

Our goal is to provide developers, designers, artists, and anyone curious about AI-generated art with an interactive space that integrates exploration, learning, and creation.

# Feature Overview
## 1. Homepage
The homepage is the first stop for users entering Civision, showcasing community-curated models, works, and the latest activity information.

You can also browse and share popular community resources such as models, assets, and workflows. Additionally, you can upload your own local models and works to co-create with other community members.

![image.png](./_resources/en/AIGC_home_page.png)

## 2. Inspiration Hub
The Inspiration Hub is where ideas converge. Here, users can publish their original works and explore other creators' inspiring content. Through likes, comments, and shares, we foster the spread and development of creative ideas.

Additionally, the community regularly hosts themed challenges, encouraging everyone to create around specific concepts or problems, jointly exploring unknown territories and driving the entire community forward.

![image.png](./_resources/en/AIGC_inspiration.png)

## 3. Models
The Models section serves as the core resource center of Civision, gathering high-quality AI models provided by both the community and professional teams. Each model detail page includes comprehensive descriptions and version information, with support for online experience.

We encourage users to train or upload their own open-source models on ModelScope to promote technical exchange and collaboration, jointly advancing the development and application of AI technology.

>You can filter "My Contributions" and "My Favorites" in the top-left corner, and switch between different model types and versions for viewing.

![image.png](./_resources/en/AIGC_models.png)

For detailed instructions on uploading Civision models, please refer to [Model Upload](./model-upload.md):

## 4. Image Generation
Image generation is one of the core features of Civision, divided into two parts: 1. Quick Image Generation and 2. Professional Image Generation.

**4.1 Quick Image Generation**: Provides an intuitive and user-friendly interface where users simply need to input a brief text description (supporting multiple languages!), and can generate high-quality images in corresponding styles with one click.

Quick image generation supports multiple preset styles and customizable parameter adjustments, catering to diverse needs from beginners to advanced users.

![image.png](./_resources/en/image_generation_simple_mode.png)

**4.2 Professional Image Generation**: When basic quick image generation is no longer sufficient to spark your inspiration, you can seamlessly transition to the professional image generation page to unlock unlimited possibilities.

In professional mode, we support more powerful prompt functionality, richer base model and LoRA combinations, and have introduced advanced capabilities such as image-to-image generation, high-resolution restoration, and ControlNet—perfect for AI image creators with more specialized requirements.

![image.png](./_resources/en/image_generation_advanced_mode.png)

>For detailed tutorials, please refer to [Quick Image Generation](./quick-image-generation.md) and [Professional Image Generation](./professional-image-generation.md).

## 5. Video Generation
Our newly launched video generation feature currently supports text-to-video generation, with plans to unlock image-to-video functionality in the future.

After entering your prompt, you can utilize our powerful prompt optimization tools to customize and refine prompts based on different base models, combined with rich LoRA combinations to create unique and creative videos.

![image.png](./_resources/en/video_generation.png)

## 6. Model Training
For users seeking deeper personalization, the model training page enables you to train custom LoRA models using your uploaded datasets based on existing Civision base models.

Currently, we support LoRA training for both **image generation** and **video generation** models.

With this feature, you can create unique LoRA models online that reflect your personal style. Whether crafting distinctive signature styles or achieving specific scene rendering effects, the model training feature will be your trusted assistant! 💗

![image.png](./_resources/en/model_train.png)

>For detailed tutorials, please refer to [Model Training](./model-training.md).

## 8. Resource Library
The Resource Library is your centralized management platform for all generated or uploaded content within Civision, designed to help you conveniently manage all your required assets.

It not only integrates convenient publishing functionality, allowing works to be easily shared to the Inspiration Hub or sent to other users, but also provides an intuitive interface to help users properly organize, efficiently search, and easily edit different types of resource materials.

![image.png](./_resources/en/AIGC_resources.png)

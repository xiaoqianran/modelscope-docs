**ModelScope aims to build the next-generation open-source Model-as-a-Service sharing platform, providing flexible, easy-to-use, and cost-effective one-stop model services for AI developers, making model applications simpler!**

ModelScope was officially launched in early November 2022 as an open-source model community and innovation platform, initiated by Alibaba's Tongyi Lab in collaboration with the CCF [Open Source Development Technical Committee](https://www.ccf.org.cn/kyfzwyh/). ModelScope partners with domestic AI industry collaborators and academic institutions to promote open-source deep learning models and innovative model service technologies through open community collaboration, driving the prosperity of the model application ecosystem.

We hope to aggregate industry-leading pre-trained models, reduce redundant R&D costs for developers, and provide a more environmentally friendly, open-source AI development environment and model services to support the development of green "digital economy" initiatives. The ModelScope platform will provide various high-quality models in an open-source manner, allowing developers to freely experience and download them on the platform.

You can learn more about [About Us](./ModelScope Community/About Us.md) or get started quickly with ModelScope through the [Quick Start](./Getting Started/Quick Start.md) guide.

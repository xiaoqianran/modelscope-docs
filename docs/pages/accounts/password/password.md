# Password Modification and Password Recovery

This article introduces account center related functions, including forgetting password, changing password, modifying bound phone number, and other operations.

First, ModelScope community account registration requires binding a phone number. If you haven't bound a phone number in previous versions, please complete phone number binding according to platform prompts after logging in.

## Change Password
Click avatar - Personal Center<br />![image.png](./_resources/1.png)<br />Enter the Personal Center page, click the edit button above the avatar to access the Account Center page.<br />![image.png](./_resources/2.png)<br />Click "Change Login Password"<br />![image.png](./_resources/3.png)<br />Follow the page prompts to complete identity verification and password modification.<br />![image.png](./_resources/4.png)

![image.png](./_resources/5.png)<br />After successful modification, you will be automatically redirected to the login page to log in with your new username and password.<br />![image.png](./_resources/6.png)

## Forgot Password
If you forget your login password, you can click the "Forgot Password" button in the bottom right corner of the login interface and reset your password according to the prompts.<br />![image.png](./_resources/7.png)<br />![image.png](./_resources/8.png)<br />Enter the verification code to reset your password<br />![image.png](./_resources/9.png)

Note: If your account is not bound to a phone number, you cannot recover your password. We recommend that you register a new account and bind a phone number.<br />![image.png](./_resources/10.png)

## Modify Bound Phone Number
If you need to modify your bound phone number, go to the **Personal Center - Account Settings** page, click the "Modify Bound Phone Number" button, and follow the prompts to change your bound phone number.<br />![image.png](./_resources/11.png)<br />Note: Changing your bound phone number requires verification. A verification code will be sent to your original phone number. If verification is successful, you can bind a new phone number. If your original phone number is lost or unavailable, you cannot verify your identity. In this case, we recommend contacting us or registering a new account to continue using ModelScope.<br />![image.png](./_resources/12.png)

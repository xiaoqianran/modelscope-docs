This document aims to guide you through the process of creating a new organization on the ModelScope platform.

# How to Apply and Get Verified
To ensure your organization creation application proceeds smoothly, please follow the guidelines below to fill out the [Organization Application Form](https://modelscope.cn/organization/create).

Information to be filled includes:
   - Organization Chinese Name: The display name of your organization. Ensure it accurately reflects your organization's characteristics and positioning.
   - Organization English Name: Limited to English letters and numbers, cannot start with a number. This will be used to create code repositories.
   - Organization Type: Can be filled as School/Research Institution/Enterprise
   - Related Links: Feel free to associate your organization's official website or your team's GitHub address and other links
   - Contact Information: The method for us to contact you later. You can provide email/phone number or other contact information
   - Detailed Introduction: Enter introduction content to help more people understand and get to know your organization. Rich text content input is supported.

# Application Success
If your organization creation application is approved, you will receive an email reply. After logging into your ModelScope account, you can see your created organization in your personal console.

# Overview
This guide provides detailed instructions on how to register as a member of the ModelScope community, as well as how to log in and manage your personal account information.

# Account Registration
### Registration Methods
You can access the registration page by clicking the "Log in/Register" button in the top right corner of the ModelScope homepage.
![image.png](./_resources/注册.png)

You need to fill in the following registration information:
- Username: Set your desired username, 6-20 characters, letters or numbers, starting with a letter.
- Phone Number: Enter your phone number, ensuring it is accurate to receive the verification code.
- Verification Code: After clicking the "Send" button, the system will send a text message containing a numeric verification code to your provided phone number. Correctly enter the received verification code in the designated input field and ensure completion within the valid time period.
- Login Password: For account security, please set a strong password. We recommend including uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters, with a minimum length of 6 characters.
- Read and agree to the [ModelScope Community Account Service Agreement](https://modelscope.cn/protocol/%E7%94%A8%E6%88%B7%E5%8D%8F%E8%AE%AE%E4%B8%8E%E9%9A%90%E7%A7%81%E6%94%BF%E7%AD%96)

After completing the above steps, click the "Agree and Register" button. If the information is correct, the system will prompt you that registration was successful and automatically redirect you to the login page.

# Account Login
### Username/Phone Number Login
On the login page, you need to enter the username or phone number used during registration, along with the corresponding authentication information:
![image.png](./_resources/登录.png)
After clicking "Login," the system will verify your information and log you into your account.

### Third-party Login
For user convenience, we also support direct login through third-party accounts:

#### CSDN Third-party Login

Click the CSDN icon and follow the instructions to authorize and link your CSDN account to log in to ModelScope.

#### GitHub Third-party Login

Click the GitHub icon and follow the instructions to authorize and link your GitHub account to log in to ModelScope.

# Personal Center
Once your account is successfully created and logged in, you can access ModelScope's Personal Center through different entry points on the [homepage](https://modelscope.cn/my/overview). Here, you can:

- View account settings: including username, bound phone number, and other basic information.
- Modify account settings: including setting a personalized avatar, setting identity, setting contact email, and other operations.
- Account security management:
  - Forgot password: Email or phone number recovery service is provided. Follow the instructions to reset your password.
  - Change password: You can change your account password at any time in security settings to enhance account security.
  - Change bound phone number: If you need to change your bound phone number, you can modify it after verifying your original phone number or email.

The ModelScope open-source community is committed to providing you with a secure, efficient, and user-friendly platform for communication and learning. We look forward to your participation in jointly promoting technological advancement and knowledge sharing. If you encounter any issues during registration or login, please feel free to contact our customer service team for assistance.
